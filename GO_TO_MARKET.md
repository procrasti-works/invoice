# Payvio — First 10 Users Pilot Kit

Goal: get **10 paying (or pilot-committed) Windhoek SMEs** on Payvio in ~3 weeks.
That traction — not the code — is what makes Payvio fundable/acquirable.

The hook is the **NamRA e-invoicing mandate (2026–2029)**. You are not selling
"nice invoicing software." You are selling "get compliant before you're forced to,
without paying an accountant N$X/hour."

---

## 1. Who to target (in priority order)

**Best first customers = businesses that invoice a lot, hate paperwork, and fear NamRA:**

1. **Accountants & bookkeepers** (CHANNEL, not just customers) — one bookkeeper
   serves 20–50 SMEs. Land one and they bring their whole client book. Highest leverage.
2. **Trade & services SMEs** — plumbers, electricians, builders, IT shops, cleaning
   companies, printers. They invoice constantly and currently use Word/Excel/paper.
3. **Small retailers / wholesalers** with VAT registration (turnover near/over N$1M).
4. **Consultants & creative freelancers** — designers, marketers, photographers.

Avoid for now: micro-vendors with no VAT and < 5 invoices/month (won't pay), and
large companies with existing ERP (too slow to switch).

---

## 2. The pilot offer (what you actually say yes to)

> **"3 months free. In return, I want 20 minutes of honest feedback and — if you
> like it — a testimonial I can show other businesses."**

- Free for 3 months, then N$150/mo (Starter) — locked at that rate for year one.
- You personally onboard them (15 min screen-share or in person).
- No contract. They can leave anytime.

Why free first: you need proof and testimonials more than you need N$150 right now.
Convert to paid in month 3 once it's part of their workflow.

## 4. The 3-week plan

**Week 1 — line up 30 targets, send 30 messages.**
- 15 SMEs (WhatsApp) + 5 accounting firms (email) + 10 from personal network.
- Track in a simple sheet: Name · Type · Channel · Sent · Reply · Demo · Onboarded.

**Week 2 — demo + onboard everyone who replies.**
- Goal: 10 onboarded. Onboarding = create their org, add 1 real client, send 1 real
  invoice, scan 1 receipt **live** (this is your wow moment — needs OCR deployed).

**Week 3 — collect feedback + testimonials, fix top 3 complaints.**
- Ask each: "What almost made you not use this?" Fix those three things.
- Get 3 written testimonials + permission to name them.

**Success = 10 active orgs + 3 testimonials.** That's the asset you can show a buyer.

---

## 5. What to measure (the only numbers a buyer/investor cares about)

- # active organizations (logged in + sent ≥1 invoice in last 7 days)
- # invoices created / receipts scanned
- Week-2 retention (came back after onboarding)
- Pilot → paid conversion (month 3)

Ten retained businesses in a country with no NamRA-native competitor is a real story.
Zero users is not. Everything in this kit serves getting that first number off zero.
